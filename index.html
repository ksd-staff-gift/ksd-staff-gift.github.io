Thank You for Supporting Our Staff

As the school year comes to an end, Bacich and Kent families are continuing the tradition of thanking our school staff with a monetary gift.

This voluntary collection is simply an end-of-year expression of gratitude for our incredibly hard-working school staff.

🎁 Contributions will be distributed to:

✓ Kent teachers
✓ Kent office and custodial staff
✓ Bacich enrichment and specialist teachers
✓ Paraprofessionals / classroom aides

Bacich homeroom teachers are covered by a separate collection organized by KSPTA Lead Room Parents.
